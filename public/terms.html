TS Video Tracker provides channel and video performance dashboards for authorized Team Secret social and creative team members.

Authorized use

Use the service only if you have permission to access the team workspace. Connect a TikTok account only when you own it or are authorized to manage it. Each account owner or manager must complete the platform's authorization flow. Keep access credentials private and follow applicable platform terms when using or exporting data.

Service and data

The dashboard displays information supplied by connected platforms and historical snapshots collected by the app. Counters, availability and refresh times depend on those platforms, their API limits and the app's synchronization schedule. Metrics may be delayed or incomplete. Connecting an account shares its collected information with authorized workspace users.

Account removal

Removing a tracked account in Channels deletes its associated history from the active app database. Export any needed reports beforehand. You can revoke the TikTok connection in your TikTok account settings.

Platform relationship

TS Video Tracker is a separate service and is not operated or endorsed by TikTok or Google. Videos, channel identities and other content remain subject to their owners' rights and the relevant platform policies.

Support and changes

Features and availability may change as the connected platforms change. For questions about access, these terms or service issues, contact Joyce@teamsecret.gg. Data handling is described in the Privacy Policy.
