TS VIDEO TRACKER

Privacy Policy

Updated October 15, 2026

TS Video Tracker is a channel performance dashboard for Team Secret social and creative teams. This policy describes the information used by the service.

Information processed

Google sign-in supplies your email address, name and profile image to identify authorized team members. For each TikTok account you connect, the app requests your account identifier, display name, avatar, follower and like counts, video count, public video metadata and available performance counters. YouTube channel and video information is also collected through its APIs.

Purpose and access

Information is used to display channel profiles, published videos, performance trends and reports to authorized users of the shared team workspace. Connecting an account makes its collected metrics visible to those users. The TikTok connection requests read-only access to profile statistics and public videos.

Storage and providers

The app stores account metadata, video metadata and metric snapshots in its database. OAuth access and refresh tokens are encrypted in the database and processed by the server to retrieve and refresh authorized data. Vercel hosts the app; the configured database provider processes stored data. Google and TikTok process authorization and API requests under their own policies. Session cookies support sign-in.

Disconnecting and deletion

Authorized workspace users can remove a tracked account in Channels. The app deletes that account and its related history from the active database. You may also revoke the app connection in TikTok settings. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not by itself delete previously stored snapshots. Contact the app administrator for data access, correction or deletion requests. Copies in service-provider backups are subject to the provider's retention settings.
