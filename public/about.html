TS VIDEO TRACKER

Channel performance,
in one place.

TS Video Tracker helps Team Secret's social and creative teams review their connected YouTube and TikTok channels, find published videos and understand performance over time.

Connect each account

A channel owner or authorized manager signs in to TikTok and approves read-only access. The dashboard displays profile information, public video metadata and available performance counters for each connected account.

Review performance together

Authorized workspace users can browse videos, compare channel metrics and export reports. Data refreshes on the app's sync schedule and depends on each platform's API availability.
